# Questions and answers

### Is this medical advice?

Clockblock gives healthy adults general information about timing light, sleep and caffeine, based on published research.
It cannot diagnose or treat a condition. Flight crew must not use it for duty schedules.
If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or take medication, consult a doctor before changing your sleep.

### Does it need the internet?

Clockblock has no internet permission. Airport search, planning, reminders and widgets work in flight mode.

### Do I need an account?

Clockblock requires no account and has no ads or tracking.
[Settings and your data](settings-and-your-data.md#your-data) explains where Clockblock stores your data.

### How does it work?

Light changes your body clock's timing. Its effect depends on when you receive it relative to your body clock.
Clockblock estimates your body clock time and schedules light exposure and avoidance.
Plans move your clock about 1 to 1.5 hours earlier per day, or up to two hours later.
Clockblock schedules sleep, naps and caffeine around those changes.
Open **About Clockblock** for details, or a block's **Why?** for its reason.

The [research review](../docs/science.md) gives the scientific sources and limitations.

### Why does the plan ask me to wear sunglasses at noon?

Light at that moment would move your body clock against the plan's intended direction.
The effect depends on your body clock time. Tap the block's **Why?** to see its reason.

### I missed a block. Is my plan ruined?

Mark the block **Skipped** or **Can't do this**, then follow the next block.
A missed block can delay adjustment. Clockblock records your answer but keeps the existing schedule.

### My flight is delayed. What do I do?

Open the trip and tap **Edit**. Use the **I'm delayed** card, available from 48 hours before departure until arrival.
See [Planning a trip](planning-a-trip.md#when-your-flight-is-delayed).

### It's a short trip. Do I have to adapt?

For returns within 72 hours, the automatic plan keeps your body clock on its starting time.
Add your return flight so Clockblock can detect the short stay.
You can also select **Home time** under **Body clock** in the trip editor.

### Why is there no melatonin in my plan?

Melatonin is off by default. Open **Settings** → **Tools** → **Melatonin** and read the safety note to enable it.
Plans suggest melatonin only when shifting your clock earlier.
Many countries regulate melatonin as a medicine. Consult a doctor or pharmacist before taking it.

### Why did I get no reminder during the night?

During planned sleep, Clockblock sends only the wake-up reminder.

### Reminders arrive late or not at all.

Check **Settings** → **What Android allows**.
See [If reminders don't arrive](widgets-and-notifications.md#if-reminders-dont-arrive).

### Can I move my trips to a new phone?

Use **Export a backup** on the old phone and **Import a backup** on the new phone.
See [Back up and restore](settings-and-your-data.md#back-up-and-restore).

### Can I see the plan in my calendar?

Open a plan's menu and choose **Export to calendar**.

### Is the app free?

Clockblock is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.
Its code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/rock3r/clockblock).

### Are there any surprises hidden in the app?

Clockblock includes hidden animations. It disables them during planned sleep and when Settings has Reduce motion enabled.
